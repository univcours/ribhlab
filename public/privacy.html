RivoScan Privacy Policy

Updated: 12 November 2026. RivoScan is developed by RibhLab.

Documents and camera

Core scanning, PDF conversion, editing and export are handled on your device. RivoScan does not upload document pages to our servers for these core operations. Google ML Kit and Android components may be used to scan documents. Files leave the device if you choose to share them or use an optional online service.

Advertising

RivoScan may display Google AdMob advertising. Depending on your settings, applicable privacy law and consent, Google and advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, device/app information, approximate network information (such as IP address), ad impressions, interactions and diagnostic data to display, measure and protect advertising. Ads are requested only when privacy permissions allow. Ad privacy options can be reopened from within the app where available. See Google Privacy Policy.

OCR, translation, and online services

Some features download models for local recognition or translation. Optional online tools may transmit data you explicitly submit to your chosen third-party service; please read the provider's terms before sharing sensitive content.

Local data, sharing and retention

Preferences and a limited list of app actions may be stored locally. You manage exported files at their destination. Uninstalling normally clears app-local data but not exported copies. Android device settings may offer additional ad privacy controls.

Contact

Contact the developer through the verified support contact published on the RivoScan Google Play listing. Publish this policy at a verified public URL before public launch.
