# RibhLab Apps — Official Catalog

RibhLab builds practical Android utilities and games. This page is the official reference for our apps and their distribution channels.

> **Security:** Install only builds linked from the official RibhLab pages. Never install third-party APK mirrors that cannot demonstrate the same signing certificate.

## RivoScan — Document Scanner & PDF

**Android package:** `com.ribhlab.rivoscan`

Scan paper documents, organize pages and export PDFs. Official APK distribution to independent stores is being prepared; please use the official publisher channels until download links are validated.

## NovaGrid: Cosmic Block Puzzle

**Android package:** `com.novagrid.cosmicpuzzle`

A space-themed block puzzle game. Independent store distribution is being prepared.

## TradingFiew — Market Companion

An Android application for viewing financial markets. Availability and the final release build are being verified. This app does not provide guaranteed returns or financial advice.
