RivoScan Privacy Policy — Draft

RivoScan is developed by RibhLab. This policy covers the open beta implementation dated October 31, 2026, and must be reviewed and hosted at a public URL before release.

Document processing

Core PDF editing, merging, scanning, signing and conversion operate locally in your device or browser. Documents are not uploaded to our servers by these tools.

OCR language packs

Optical character recognition may download OCR language models from third-party infrastructure. The documents are processed locally once the models are available; the OCR service may receive ordinary network connection information when fetching model files.

Camera and files

Camera access is requested only when you choose to capture a document. Files are accessed only when you select them. Generated files can be shared or saved using your device's share dialog, which sends files only to destinations you choose.

Local settings

Language preference and a small list of operation names/dates are stored on your device; document contents and filenames are not intentionally saved in the local activity list. Native temporary exports may be written to the app's cache directory until cleared.

Data retention

Uninstalling the app clears app-local preferences and temporary data in normal Android behavior. Shared or exported files are managed by the destination you select.

Advertising and AI

This beta build contains no ads, analytics, accounts, cloud AI translation or subscriptions. The policy will be updated if features are added.

Contact

For inquiries, please use the developer contact channel shown in the RivoScan Google Play listing. Add a verified developer support email and public policy URL before publishing.
