RivoScan Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy applies to the RivoScan Android application (com.ribhlab.rivoscan), developed by RibhLab. This policy also has an online version.

Documents, camera and local storage

You choose the documents and images you open. The core PDF scanner, PDF editing, OCR, export and signature tools are designed to operate locally on your device. Camera access is requested when you use scanning features. RivoScan's core tools do not automatically upload your documents to an RibhLab-operated server or require a RivoScan account. The device may store selected files, exports, temporary previews, caches, language preferences and limited activity metadata. Files saved in shared device folders are controlled by your file manager and may remain after the app is uninstalled.

OCR models and scanning technologies

Google ML Kit scanner components, OCR training files or offline translation models may be downloaded when first used. Service providers may receive technical connection data, such as IP address and basic device information, during downloads. Local OCR is designed to process selected content on the device after the required models are available.

Optional cloud AI

The optional document AI features can contact OpenAI after you provide your own API key, choose the content or question and approve sending it. Selected text, images and prompts may be transmitted to OpenAI for processing. This is not required for the basic scanner or PDF tools. Only share content you are authorized to disclose. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy. External providers manage data retention under their own policies.

Advertising and consent

The Android code integrates Google AdMob and Google consent management. Developer builds show Google test-only ads; signed production builds can display consented interstitial ads only at natural pauses after completed work, and app-open ads when returning from the background. All document features remain available without watching videos or unlocking them. There are no banner ads. Google's ad services and partners may process advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device information, ad interactions, diagnostics and consent preferences. Depending on jurisdiction, you may be asked for advertising consent; privacy options are available in the app settings. See Google's advertising technologies and Google's Privacy Policy.

Optional device-to-device transfer

When you explicitly use direct file transfer, RivoScan can negotiate a WebRTC connection using connection codes shared with another device. Google's public STUN server may receive network metadata, including public IP address, to help establish the connection. Documents are sent to the chosen recipient device when the transfer takes place. Some networks do not support this feature.

Data retention, deletion and permissions

Application preferences and caches may be deleted by clearing RivoScan's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app. Files exported to shared storage must be managed there separately. You can revoke camera and file access via Android permissions. Data managed by third-party advertising, model download or AI providers is governed by their own retention and deletion policies.

Choices and security

Basic document scanning and PDF editing do not need optional AI requests or advertising videos. New scans are saved as PDFs; when you choose Share as JPG, temporary image copies are generated in the app cache and shared only after your action. You control when to share or export a document. You should avoid sending sensitive information through optional network features unless you understand the recipient and provider's data handling.
