RivoScan Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026. RivoScan is developed by RibhLab. This notice covers the Android scanner and document utility, including optional AdMob advertising when a production build is enabled.

Documents and camera

RivoScan uses camera access to scan when requested. Document scans, edits, PDF exports and OCR processing normally run locally on the device. Selected files remain on your device unless you explicitly share or transfer them. Google ML Kit scanner components or OCR language data may be downloaded to support scanning and recognition.

Advertising and consent

RivoScan integrates Google AdMob. In development builds, Google demo ads are used and do not generate revenue. In a production build, ads may include optional rewarded videos that unlock advanced tools temporarily, occasional full-screen interstitial ads at natural breaks, and app-open ads after returning to the app. No banner ads are used. Advertising and consent technology may process network information, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics as described by Google. Where required, a consent choice is requested before ads are loaded; you can revisit privacy options inside RivoScan settings.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob privacy guidance for more information.

Local settings and retention

Language, scanner preferences and limited activity metadata may be stored locally. Scans exported to shared Documents/RivoScan remain under your device's file management; deleting or uninstalling the app may not remove shared documents. Cache and preferences may be cleared when the application is uninstalled.

Other optional connections

Optional device-to-device file transfer may use internet connectivity for connection negotiation. OCR models and other selected features may connect to third-party services, while files themselves are only transferred when you choose a sharing or online feature. Review each feature before submitting sensitive documents.

Your choices

You can use basic scanning and saved documents without watching a rewarded ad. You may refuse optional rewarded ads. You can change applicable advertising privacy choices within the app and can manage or delete locally saved files using device controls.

Contact

For support and privacy requests, use the verified developer contact listed on the RivoScan Google Play listing. The developer must verify a working support contact and publish this policy at a public URL before store release.
