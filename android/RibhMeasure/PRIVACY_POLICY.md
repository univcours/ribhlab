# RibhMeasure — Privacy Policy / سياسة الخصوصية

**Effective date: November 19, 2026**

## English

RibhMeasure, published by RibhLab, helps you measure approximate distances, land area, heights and dimensions.

**Location:** GPS measurements use your approximate or precise location only while the GPS measurement screen is open. Latitude/longitude data are used locally to calculate area and distance. We do not transmit GPS measurements to a RibhLab server. The history saves calculated results and point counts, not individual GPS coordinates.

**Camera/photos:** The system camera can capture an image for a calibrated size calculation. The latest capture is stored locally in the app's cache and may be cleared by Android. The photo is not transmitted to a RibhLab server. You can discard it by clearing app storage or removing the app.

**Motion sensors:** Accelerometer measurements are processed locally for the level tool.

**History:** Calculated results are saved locally on the device. You can delete history within the app or export it as CSV. RibhLab does not receive these exports.

**Advertisements and consent:** We use (or plan to use) Google AdMob for advertising. Google's Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform may collect device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and other information according to Google's terms and your choices. Where required, the app requests advertising consent before loading ads, and offers privacy options. Advertising availability varies by country and consent status. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Data sharing and security:** The app has no RibhLab backend or accounts. Google and its advertising partners may process data to deliver and measure ads. The OS retains control over location permissions, and measurements are not intended for official surveys.

**Children:** The app is not designed for children under 13 and is not directed to children. The publisher must complete age targeting and data safety disclosures before public distribution.

**Contact:** Contact RibhLab through the official site: https://ribhlab.github.io/ .

## العربية

يساعدك تطبيق RibhMeasure التابع لـ RibhLab على قياس المسافات والمساحات والأطوال بشكل تقريبي.

**الموقع:** يُستعمل GPS فقط أثناء فتح شاشة القياس لحساب المساحة والمسافة محلياً. لا نرسل إحداثياتك إلى خادم RibhLab؛ يُحفظ في السجل الناتج وعدد النقط فقط دون إحداثياتها.

**الصور:** تُستخدم كاميرا الهاتف لالتقاط صورة بهدف قياس طول شخص أو جسم باستعمال مرجع معروف. تبقى آخر صورة في ذاكرة التخزين المؤقت للتطبيق ولا تُرسل إلى خوادمنا.

**المستشعرات:** تُستعمل بيانات التسارع محلياً لميزان الاستواء.

**السجل:** تُحفظ النتائج في هاتفك ويمكنك حذفها أو تصديرها بصيغة CSV.

**الإعلانات:** نستعمل أو نعتزم استعمال Google AdMob، والذي قد يعالج معرفات الجهاز وبيانات الاستخدام والتشخيص وفق سياسة Google وموافقتك حيث يلزم. بإمكانك مراجعة خيارات الخصوصية المتاحة داخل التطبيق. [سياسة Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
